Build a five-slide deck `security.pptx` for an all-hands.

Slide one is the title: "Security Program 2027". Then three slides: what we fixed
this year (SSO everywhere, secrets out of CI), what we are fixing next (device
trust and an audit log), and what we need from every team (rotate keys quarterly,
review access monthly). Slide five closes on the one thing to remember: least
privilege, everywhere, by default.

It is the deck we would also show a customer, so it should look finished rather
than like an internal draft.

Both `python-pptx` and `pptxgenjs` (Node) are already installed here, so use
whichever suits you and do not install anything.
