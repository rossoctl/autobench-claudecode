# Requirements for Outputs

## Every deck

These are properties of the finished `.pptx`, whichever way you build it.

### No Accent Lines Under Titles
- Never put a thin rule, underline or divider bar beneath a slide title — it is the clearest
  signal a deck was generated rather than designed.
- Separate a title from its content with whitespace or a background colour instead.

### Dark/Light Sandwich
- The **title slide and the closing slide get a dark background**; every content slide between
  them gets a **light** one.
- Text colour follows the background: light text on the dark slides, dark text on the light ones.
