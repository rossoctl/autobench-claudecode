    even when the skill states the rule.
  * opus-5 OFF xlsx-fin-colors = 0.20 -- it sometimes knows the convention
    with no skill at all.
So these tasks DO rank models, below and above the sonnet band. Read pass rate
as a real measurement, not as a formality.
